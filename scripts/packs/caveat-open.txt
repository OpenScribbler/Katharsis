# caveat-open.txt — feeds r17-caveat-unblocked in detect-reply.sh.
# One regex alternative per line, spliced into \b(?:...)\b, matched case-insensitively.
# A `C` line matching one of these says a case is left open, unfixed, or unchecked.
# Picked 2026-11-09 against 169 hand-labeled caveat lines in the ledger: with
# caveat-reasons.txt it flags 33, 18 of them a defect or check the reply could have
# settled that turn, and finds 18 of the 29 such lines.
# A missing pack disables r17.

still (?:open|fails|failing|broken|doesn't|does not|has no|lacks|missing)
not (?:yet )?(?:fixed|tested|checked|confirmed|measured|verified|run|built)
(?:un|never )(?:fixed|tested|confirmed|measured|verified)
no (?:test|direct test|negative test|end-to-end test)
nothing (?:tests|proves|exercises)
haven't (?:tested|run|checked|measured|confirmed|verified|built|added|timed)
